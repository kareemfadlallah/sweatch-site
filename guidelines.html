Community guidelines

Sweatch works because people turn up and are decent to each other. That's the whole rulebook, but here it is spelled out.

Turn up

If you joined, come. If you can't, leave the session as early as you can so someone else can have the spot. A no-show costs the host, the others, and your reliability.

Be kind

Everyone here was a beginner once. No shouting, no mocking, no drama over a lost point.

No harassment. None.

No insults, threats, slurs or hate — about gender, religion, nationality, sexuality, anything. Not in a session, not in a chat.

Keep it clean

No nudity, nothing sexual, nothing violent, nothing illegal. Not in a photo, not on a profile, not in a session, not in a chat.

Say hi once

You can say hi to someone once. If they don't answer, that is the answer. The app won't let you send another, and a message that isn't wanted is harassment.

Respect the room

A women-only session is women-only. A men-only session is men-only. Don't try to work around it.

Be real

Your name, your photo, your level. Don't inflate your level — it wastes everyone's evening.

No selling

No adverts, no coaching pitches, no collecting numbers. If you're a coach or a venue, email us instead: support@sweatch.app.

If someone breaks this

Report them first, from their profile (⋯ → Report) — once you block someone, their profile is no longer available to you. Then block them: the app stops showing you to each other in search, lists and messages. You can also email safety@sweatch.app. Accounts that break these rules get removed.
